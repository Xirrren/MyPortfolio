Independently developed / 2D scrolling platformer


這是我第一次開發到的2D卷軸平台跳躍類遊戲，也是一堂課的期末作業，在開發中我學到很多，尤其是相機Cinemachine及動畫的運用。

操作方式:

Controls:
Use A and D to move, and Space to jump.
Reach the teleport stone to proceed to the next level.

泡泡派對 BubbleParty!

BubbleParty!

團隊開發 / 2D雙人競爭地盤 / GGJ

Team Development / 2D Two-Player Battlegrounds / GGJ


這是我第一次挑戰在3天內總共5個人做出一款遊戲，主題是「泡泡」。我負責場景美術和一些 UI 轉場動畫。雖然時間很緊，但也正因為這樣，我學到怎麼快速把想法做出來，也體驗到短時間內團隊一起完成作品的感覺。

This was my first time joining a five-person team to create a game in just three days, with the theme “Bubbles.” I was in charge of environment art and some UI transition animations. Even though the time was tight, I learned how to quickly turn ideas into reality and really felt the excitement of working together to finish a project in such a short time.

操作方式:
玩家1（移動）：WASD
玩家2（移動）：IJKL
簡介:
這是一款雙人對戰遊戲，玩家可選擇成為強大的污垢終結者，或是挑戰清潔極限的髒亂之王。無論你化身哪一方，都能在這場緊張刺激的對決中一較高下。準備迎戰，看看誰能掌控這場清潔大戰！

Controls:
Player 1 (Movement) : WASD
Player 2 (Movement) : IJKL
Introduction:
This is a two-player battle game where players can choose to become the mighty Dirt Eliminator or the ultimate King of Chaos who challenges the limits of cleanliness. No matter which side you take, you can compete in this intense and exciting showdown. Get ready to face off and see who can dominate the Cleanliness War!


光是送餐都來不及了

We can't even keep up with the food delivery schedule.

團隊開發 / 2D雙人競爭 / FGJ

Team Development / 2D Two-Player Competition / FGJ


結合主題「vibe」。這次挑戰擔任程式設計，主要負責 遊戲中送餐系統的實作。由於是第一次在有限時間內開發程式，過程中必須快速理清邏輯與層級關係，雖然一度手忙腳亂， 但也讓我更了解如何在壓力下保持思考並完成功能。

Combined with the theme "vibe". I took on the challenge of programming and was mainly responsible for implementing the food delivery system. Since it was my first time coding under a tight deadline, I had to quickly organize the logic and hierarchy. Although it was a bit hectic at times, it helped me learn how to stay composed and complete features under pressure.

操作方式:
玩家1（移動）：WASD (衝刺):空白鍵
玩家2（移動）：上下左右 (衝刺):Control鍵
簡介:
兩位玩家可以互相爭奪餐點，如果撞到對方玩家可以偷對方拿到的餐點，時間內獲得送餐次數最多的玩家獲勝。

Controls:
Player 1 (Move): WASD (Sprint): Spacebar
Player 2 (Move): Up, Down, Left, Right (Sprint): Control key
The two players can compete for food. If they bump into the other player, they can steal the food the other player has. The player who delivers the most food within the time limit wins.

海豹呼呼 Seal Breath

Seal Breath

團隊開發 / 音樂節奏 / FGJ

Team Development / Music Rhythm / FGJ


本作品為「全 AI 協作開發」的創新嘗試。 除部分美術外，程式與流程皆由 AI 主導。我們以「不直接操作 Unity 介面」為原則，透過持續的成果反饋 與迭代微調，順利完成開發。

This project is an innovative experiment in full AI-assisted development. Aside from select art assets, the code and overall pipeline were completely driven by AI. Adopting an approach that minimized direct manipulation within the Unity interface, we brought the project to completion through iterative output evaluation and continuous fine-tuning.

操作方式:
使用F和J鍵上下移動呼呼。 跟隨音樂的節奏，控制呼呼的動作來捕魚和躍出冰洞。
簡介:
海豹呼呼肚子餓了，要下海釣魚吃。下海前必須先吸一大口氣，在釣魚期間一定要衝出冰川呼吸。 不同的氧氣量和飽食度會引發不同的結局。

Controls:
Use the F and J keys to move Huhu up and down. Follow the rhythm of the music and control Huhu’s movement to eat fish and leap out of the ice cave.
Introduction:
Huhu the seal is hungry and needs to dive into the ocean to catch some fish. Before diving, Huhu must take a deep breath. While hunting underwater, Huhu also needs to leap out of the water through openings in the ice to breathe. Different levels of oxygen and satiety will lead to different endings.
